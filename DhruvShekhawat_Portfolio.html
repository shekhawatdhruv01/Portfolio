Case Study

AfterBell

Self-initiated Product Case Study · Jan–Feb 2026
AfterBell — Fixing the coordination chaos behind after-school activities.

Urban working parents aren't struggling to find activities for their kids — they're drowning in the operational friction that follows: WhatsApp groups for scheduling, cash or bank transfers for payments, phone calls to confirm slots, and no single place to track it all. The discovery problem is just as real — without a proper way to evaluate options, most parents default to wherever a friend's kid is going or what the neighbour's child attends, regardless of whether it's the right fit. Meanwhile, coaches and independent instructors are managing batches on paper, chasing payment follow-ups, and losing students to poor visibility.

AfterBell is a two-sided marketplace that replaces this friction with a structured, digital-first experience — intelligent booking flows, transparent pricing with tax breakdowns, persistent schedule dashboards for parents, and batch + attendance tools for coaches. The MVP was scoped to validate core booking behaviour before expanding scope, and a working prototype was built end-to-end using Google AI Studio to simulate real user flows.
